# SaaS Market 2026 — Key Findings

_Note: the 'dex' category is excluded from all price comparisons — its listed prices are swap-fee percentages, not USD/month subscription costs (see data quality notes)._

## Pricing
- Median entry-level paid price across the market: **$16.00/mo**.
- Most expensive category to enter: **Crypto Analytics** (avg $153.71/mo).
- Cheapest category to enter: **Password Managers** (avg $2.79/mo).

## Free-Tier Strategy
- 74.3% of all 331 products offer some kind of free plan.
- Highest free-tier adoption: **DeFi Tools** (100%).
- Lowest free-tier adoption: **VPN** (20%).

## Does Price Predict Quality?
- Correlation between entry price and rating: r = -0.014 (p = 0.823) — **not statistically significant** at α = 0.05.
- In plain terms: paying more does not reliably buy a higher-rated product in this market.

## Features vs Price
- Correlation between feature count and entry price: r = -0.078 (p = 0.206).

## Rating by Category
- One-way ANOVA across categories (min. 5 rated products each): F = 5.29, p = 0.0000 — differences in average rating across categories are **statistically significant**.

## Best Value (rating per dollar) — Top 5
- **Bitwarden** (Password Managers): 4.75 rating at $1.00/mo
- **Remove.bg** (AI Image Generators): 4.75 rating at $2.00/mo
- **Namecheap** (Cloud Hosting): 4.5 rating at $1.98/mo
- **NordPass** (Password Managers): 4.4 rating at $1.99/mo
- **Pixlr** (Design Tools): 4.4 rating at $1.99/mo

## Billing Models
- Distribution across all 1,013 pricing plans:
  - flat: 79.3%
  - per-seat: 17.0%
  - usage-based: 3.2%
  - per-project: 0.6%
